    Roadmap to platinum — about 200 hours, 1 playthrough

    1. Step 1 — Play the story, save every chapter (60–80 hours). Finish Chapters 1–5 and the final mission. Make a manual save at the start of each chapter (15 manual saves + 1 autosave exist). Before you leave Chapter 1, meditate at the balcony spot: Positive Mental Attitude is the only trophy flagged as potentially missable, and the Chapter 1 spot may not be reachable later. All other story trophies are unmissable.
    2. Step 2 — 100% completion + challenges. After the story, chase A Long Road: finish all Strangers & Freaks strands (8 needed), all activities (shooting range golds, drone races, races, fishing, arcade, bowling, meditation), 50 challenges, 250 stunts, 100 vehicles discovered, full exploration of Leonida and the collectibles (3 card sets, WAiNK at 100 locations).
    3. Step 3 — Cleanup: robberies, money & misc. Rob 5 bank vaults, an armored truck, 3 shops back-to-back, a jewelry store undetected and one illegal operation per gang; launder $250,000 in Marked Bills, spend $100,000, buy all garages, register 10 vehicles; then the cryptic misc trophies (Personal Best, Actions Speak Louder, Bad Luck I Guess, The Truth Is Out There) — their triggers are not yet pinned down by any source, so check the cited guides for updates instead of guessing.

    Missable trophies

    Positive Mental Attitude — potentially missable. Meditate at every spot across Leonida, including the balcony spot in Chapter 1. No source has confirmed the full spot list or whether the Chapter 1 spot is reachable later, so keep a Chapter 1 manual save until you confirm it. Every other trophy can be earned after the story in free roam.

    Rules that protect your run

    • No online trophies and no difficulty trophy — play on any difficulty.
    • Cheats disable trophies (and the save) — do not use them on your platinum save.
    • There is no mission replay and no New Game+ — chapter saves are your only rewind.
    • Kind and missable flags here are this guide's classification from the official descriptions, not PSN data.
