    Roadmap to platinum — about 200 hours, 1 playthrough

    Step 1 — Play the story, save every chapter

    60–80 hours · 12 story trophies

    Finish Chapters 1–5 and the final mission. Make a manual save at the start and end of each chapter (15 manual saves + 1 autosave exist — 10 saves cover the chapters, rotate the other 5 in free roam). Before you leave Chapter 1, meditate at the balcony spot: Positive Mental Attitude is the only trophy flagged as potentially missable, and the Chapter 1 spot may not be reachable later. All other story trophies are unmissable, and quest givers who vanish for a while always return.

    Step 2 — 100% completion + challenges

    ~100 hours · A Long Road (gold)

    After the story, open the checklist under Progress > Stats > 100% Completion. It is closer to 60% true completion: any 100 optional missions, scan 200 of 277 base-model vehicles with WAiNK, study 100 of 255 animals with binoculars, any 70 of 80 challenges, 300 stunts, all shooting ranges, all drone races, all 8 bank jobs and all 39 gang operations. Nothing on it is missable — finish it all in free roam. This step also pops most activity trophies on the way.

    Step 3 — Cleanup: robberies, money & misc

    ~10 hours · robbery, money & misc

    Rob 5 bank vaults, an armored truck, 3 shops back-to-back, a jewelry store undetected and one illegal operation per gang; launder $250,000 in Marked Bills, spend $100,000, buy all 8 garages ($160,000), register 10 vehicles; then the cryptic misc trophies. Two of them are now pinned down — Personal Best (max strength, both characters) and Actions Speak Louder (500 profile actions) — while In The Beginning…, Bad Luck, I Guess, The Truth Is Out There and Ten Pin are still unconfirmed: check the cited guides for updates instead of guessing.

    Missable trophies — 1 of 55

    Positive Mental Attitude — potentially missable. Meditate at every spot across Leonida (18 spots, 10 locations), including the balcony spot in Chapter 1: after the mission “Down to Earth”, Lucia talks about meditating in the kitchen in the morning, then walks to the balcony at Jason’s house — follow her and accept. It adds +1 to the counter but is not listed as a spot, so it is still unconfirmed whether it is required. Do it anyway and keep a Chapter 1 manual save until the trophy pops. Every other trophy can be earned after the story in free roam.

    Rules that protect your run

    • No online trophies and no difficulty trophy — play on any difficulty.
    • Cheats disable trophies (and the save) — do not use them on your platinum save.
    • There is no mission replay and no New Game+ — chapter saves are your only rewind.
    • Optional missions are plentiful (200–300+), so a few missable ones never lock you out of the 100 you need.
    • Kind, chapter and missable flags here are this guide’s classification from the official descriptions, not PSN data.
