    Roadmap to platinum — about 200 hours, 1 playthrough

    Step 1 — Play the story, save every chapter

    60–80 hours · 12 story trophies

    Finish Chapters 1–5 and the final mission. Make a manual save at the start and end of each chapter (15 manual saves + 1 autosave). Before you leave Chapter 1, meditate at the balcony spot: Positive Mental Attitude is the only trophy flagged as potentially missable. All other story trophies are unmissable.

    Step 2 — 100% completion + challenges

    ~100 hours · A Long Road (gold)

    After the story, open Progress > Stats > 100% Completion: any 100 optional missions, 200 of 277 vehicles scanned with WAiNK, 100 of 255 animals studied, any 70 of 80 challenges, 300 stunts, all ranges, drone races, 8 bank jobs, 39 gang operations. Nothing is missable — finish it in free roam.

    Step 3 — Cleanup: robberies, money & misc

    ~10 hours · robbery, money & misc

    Rob 5 bank vaults, an armored truck, 3 shops back-to-back, a jewelry store undetected and one illegal operation per gang; launder $250,000 in Marked Bills, spend $100,000, buy all 8 garages ($160,000), register 10 vehicles; then the cryptic misc trophies. Four are still unconfirmed (In The Beginning…, Bad Luck, I Guess, The Truth Is Out There, Ten Pin) — check the cited guides instead of guessing.

    Missable trophies — 1 of 55

    Positive Mental Attitude — potentially missable. Meditate at every spot across Leonida (18 spots, 10 locations), including the balcony spot in Chapter 1: after the mission “Down to Earth”, follow Lucia to the balcony and accept. It adds +1 to the counter but is not listed as a spot, so it is still unconfirmed whether it is required — do it anyway and keep a Chapter 1 manual save until the trophy pops. Every other trophy is safe after the story; the full chapter-by-chapter breakdown is on the Missables tab.

    Rules that protect your run

    • No online trophies and no difficulty trophy — play on any difficulty.
    • Cheats disable trophies (and the save) — do not use them on your platinum save.
    • There is no mission replay and no New Game+ — chapter saves are your only rewind.
    • Optional missions are plentiful (200–300+), so a few missable ones never lock you out of the 100 you need.
    • Kind, chapter and missable flags here are this guide’s classification from the official descriptions, not PSN data.

    About & sources

    Guides are written in this tracker’s own words from the official trophy descriptions and the public guides below — never copied from them. Concrete counts, unlock conditions and in-game tracking menus come from PowerPyx’s roadmap (meditation spots, stunt categories, vehicle and animal counts, challenge totals, quest names and money figures). Every guide is numbered steps, and every step that states a fact ends with a marker [n] pointing at that trophy’s numbered source list: [1] PowerPyx for the concrete method, [2] Exophase + [3] PSNProfiles for the official name, description and grade facts they mirror. A step with no marker says plainly that no source has pinned the method down. One open question is shown both ways instead of settled by guessing: whether the Chapter 1 balcony meditation is required for Positive Mental Attitude. Where no source has pinned down a method, the guide says so. Trophy names, descriptions and grades are reproduced exactly as PlayStation publishes them (mirrored by Exophase and PSNProfiles); Rockstar Games has never published the list itself. This tracker is an unofficial fan guide: no Rockstar logo or official artwork is used anywhere.
